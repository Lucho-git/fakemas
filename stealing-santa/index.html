Stealing Santa

The Rules

8 gifts · 8 people · 2 steals · 1 die

  1. Put the eight wrapped gifts in the middle. Everyone draws a number from 1 to 8.
  2. Number 1 goes first and opens any wrapped gift.
  3. On your turn, open a wrapped gift or steal a gift that someone else has already opened.
  4. To steal, roll the die. 3, 4 or 5: the gift is yours. 6: it is yours and locked, so nobody can take it. 1 or 2: the steal fails and you open a wrapped gift instead.
  5. If your gift is stolen, you go again straight away: steal a different gift (same roll) or open a wrapped one.
  6. A gift can change hands only once per turn, so nobody can snatch theirs straight back.
  7. A gift can be stolen twice. After the second steal it is locked too, and stays where it is.
  8. A turn ends when a wrapped gift is opened; then the next number plays. The game is over when all eight are open.
